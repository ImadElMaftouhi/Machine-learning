# Classic AI / ML / Data Science Workflow

1. Problem definition
   - Clarify the business goal, the research question, and success criteria.
   - Define scope, constraints, and target metrics.

2. Data collection
   - Gather structured and unstructured data from internal and external sources.
   - Ensure data privacy, compliance, and access rights.

3. Data exploration and preprocessing
   - Inspect data quality, distributions, and missing values.
   - Clean, normalize, handle outliers, and impute missing values.

4. Feature engineering and selection
   - Create, transform, and select the most relevant features.
   - Reduce dimensionality and encode categorical variables.

5. Model selection and training
   - Choose candidate algorithms and training strategies.
   - Train models using cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning.

6. Evaluation and validation
   - Evaluate performance on hold-out and validation sets.
   - Check for bias, variance, robustness, and business alignment.

7. Deployment
   - Package the model for production: API, batch, or embedded deployment.
   - Integrate with existing systems and automate inference.

8. Monitoring and maintenance
   - Track model performance, data drift, and operational metrics.
   - Retrain, update, or retire models as conditions change.

9. Iteration and communication
   - Review results with stakeholders and iterate on the workflow.
   - Document assumptions, decisions, and outcomes.
